SheSafe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-04

1. What We Collect

  • Account identifiers: provider UID, optional email, display name.
  • Safety telemetry during active journeys: approximate or precise location, battery level, sound state, and last-online timestamps.
  • Journey context updates: text tied to a specific journey.
  • SOS evidence media: audio, photos, and video are not collected in the MVP.
  • Event logs: safety events for both primary and close-one recipients, including delivery and acknowledgement status.

2. Why We Collect It

  • Deliver safety functionality such as live tracking, check-ins, and SOS escalation.
  • Enable close-one collaboration and visibility.
  • Investigate incidents and improve false-positive rates.
  • Meet legal and store compliance requirements.

3. Data Retention

  • Active journey telemetry is retained for operational safety and incident review.
  • Completed journey context is retained for auditability until a user deletion request is processed.
  • Safety-event delivery and audit records are retained for 90 days unless account deletion removes relevant actor or recipient data earlier.
  • Invite codes expire after 24 hours and can be used once.
  • Account deletion removes account-owned profile, trusted-contact, journey, context, check-in, invite, and live-location records through a server-side deletion flow.

4. Sharing

  • Data is shared only with explicitly connected close ones for relevant journeys or groups.
  • No public sharing.
  • Third-party processors include Firebase services and map/geocoding providers used to operate the app.
  • Public launch is blocked until processor DPAs, subprocessors, and EU/UK international-transfer mechanisms are documented.

5. User Controls

  • Users can remove close ones.
  • Users can block or report a close one or submitted context through in-app controls and support escalation.
  • Users can request account deletion from the in-app Account & Privacy section.

6. Security

  • Access is protected by Firebase Auth and security rules.
  • Event/action permissions are role-scoped and journey-scoped.
  • Sensitive actions are logged with actor, recipient, and timestamp.
